# Visual Generation & Optimization Guide

## Hyper-Scientific Diagram Generation

### Diagram Types & Tools

**1. Spectrograms & Wavelength Visualizations**
- **Tool:** Veo 3.1, Runway 4.5
- **Specs:** 1248x1248px, 300 DPI, PNG/SVG
- **Elements:** Wavelength scale, absorption peaks, molecular structures, color-coded regions
- **Prompt:** "Hyper-scientific chlorophyll absorption spectrum with precise wavelength data, molecular structure, detailed annotations, laboratory-grade visualization"

**2. Anatomical & Botanical Diagrams**
- **Tool:** Da Vinci.ai, Artspace.ai
- **Specs:** 1248x1248px, 300 DPI, cross-sections, color-coded tissues
- **Elements:** Labels, measurements, tissue types, functional zones
- **Prompt:** "Ultra-detailed botanical illustration showing [structure] with cross-sections, color-coded tissues, nutrient zones, and anatomical precision"

**3. Molecular & Cellular Signaling**
- **Tool:** Veo 3.1, Dream.ai
- **Specs:** 1248x1248px, 300 DPI, particle effects, glow
- **Elements:** Ion channels, proteins, signaling cascades, cytoskeleton
- **Prompt:** "Advanced molecular visualization showing [mechanism] with stretch-activated ion channels, calcium influx, MAPK signaling cascades, intricate detail"

**4. Macro Photography & Crystal Structures**
- **Tool:** Runway 4.5, Uncensored.ai
- **Specs:** 1248x1248px, 300 DPI, crystalline formations, bioluminescence
- **Elements:** Internal structures, molecular formations, glowing compounds
- **Prompt:** "Hyper-macro crystalline visualization of [structure] with glowing molecules, internal crystalline formations, bioluminescent effects"

### Quality Standards

**Resolution:**
- Minimum: 1248x1248px (1:1 square)
- Preferred: 2496x2496px (4K equivalent)
- Print: 300 DPI minimum

**Color Accuracy:**
- Use scientifically accurate colors (not stylized)
- Include color legend/scale
- Ensure accessibility (colorblind-friendly)

**Annotations:**
- Clear, readable labels
- Measurement scales
- Legend for symbols/colors
- Source attribution

## Cinematic Hero Visuals

### Specifications

**Format:** 16:9 widescreen  
**Resolution:** 2560x1440px minimum (1440p)  
**Preferred:** 3840x2160px (4K)  
**File Format:** PNG, WebP, or JPG (optimized)

- Depth of field (subject sharp, background soft)
- Particle effects (pollen, light particles)

- Atmospheric effects (fog, light diffusion)

**3. Color Palette**
- Deep blacks (background)
- Metallic golds (accents)
- Soft greens (plant)
- Cyan/violet (energy effects)

## Asset Optimization

### For Web
- Format: WebP (primary), JPG (fallback)
- Compression: 70-85% quality
- Size: <500KB for hero images
- Lazy loading: Implement for gallery images

### For Print
- Format: PDF or high-res PNG
- Resolution: 300 DPI minimum
- Color space: CMYK (for print)
- Bleed: +0.25" on all sides

### For Email
- Format: JPG or PNG
- Size: <100KB
- Width: 600px (standard email width)
- Alt text: Descriptive for accessibility
