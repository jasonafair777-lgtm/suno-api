### B. The Operational Layer: Advanced Master Skill & AI Commons
CivicHub requires different AI models for different civic problems. Your **AI Commons Directory** and **Advanced Master Skill** provide the routing logic.

- **Capability:** The **Nominatim** skill translates text-based addresses and civic reports into exact bounding boxes and coordinates via OpenStreetMap.

### D. The Funding & Distribution Layer: Gumroad & KDP
Civic solutions require funding and dissemination.
- **Capability:** The **Hyper-Cinematic Publishing** and **Gumroad Setup** skills allow CivicHub to instantly publish its findings.

### E. The Interface: Manus Connector Hub (PWA)
- **Capability:** The `manus-hub.html` and `sw.js` files provide a Progressive Web App framework.
- **Functionality:** A mobile-ready, offline-capable interface where citizens can report issues, and city managers can view the cross-domain AI analysis.

2. **Open311 & Municipal API Integration:**
   - *Functionality:* Automatically ingesting citizen complaints (potholes, graffiti, noise) directly from existing city databases.
